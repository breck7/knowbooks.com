KNOWBOOKS FAQ

Knowbooks are books you do.

A Knowbook is an experiment kit in a book. Open one, do the experiments, and learn how things work.

Frequently asked questions

Who are Knowbooks for?

Knowbooks are for curious people of all ages. Younger children can explore them with an adult, while teenagers and adults can work through experiments independently.

What comes inside each Knowbook?

Each Knowbook contains a curated collection of real objects plus experiment cards that show you what to do to learn a superpower.

How is the current build quality?

Our first Knowbooks are Trailblazer editions. Each one is hand-crafted on Oahu. They are complete and usable, but their objects, experiments, packaging, and instructions are improving as we learn from our first customers.

Can I use a Knowbook more than once?

Yes. Knowbooks are designed to last a lifetime and can be explored repeatedly, shared, and returned to the shelf. Most objects are reusable; some contain consumable/replaceable materials.

When will my KnowBook be ready (Hawai'i customers)?

Oahu customers will generally receive their KnowBooks within 10 days, by hand delivery or pickup.

When will my Knowbook(s) ship (Mainland customers)?

Orders begin assembly immediately and typically ship within 15 business days from Hawai'i. You'll receive tracking when your order is on its way. Delivery times will improve as we scale up.
